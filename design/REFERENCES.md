## Aesthetic goals
Cinematic neo-brutalism: geometric precision plus expressive photography, analog grain, obsidian/bone/crimson/oxblood palette, sparse neon and motion with purpose.

## Distinct pages
- Discover: mood-driven exploration instead of a generic recommendation feed.
- Compose: visual moodspace editor, with controlled placement and useful focus.
- Listen: immersive and distraction-minimal layout, with queue/controls accessible on demand.

## Safety and copyright
These screenshots contain third-party application imagery, artwork and interfaces. Do not commit them to public GitHub or use the images themselves in the deployed product; use original or permissively licensed media for app assets.

## Quality checklist
- Legible labels/text and obvious clickable targets.
- Keyboard accessible controls, visible focus, sensible tab order.
- Correct loading, empty, error, success states.
- Reduce-motion mode and safe mobile scaling.
- Avoid uncontrolled use of red/neon and excessive glassmorphism.
